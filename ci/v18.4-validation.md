# V18.4 validation

Completed Binance USD-M BTC and ETH candles. Decisions sampled every two hours from 2026-10-09 through 2026-10-29 UTC with August–October candle warm-up. Both releases received the same archived candles. OI, funding and order-book history were unavailable for this replay. No account orders were placed.

| Measure | V18.3.1 | V18.4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Idea alerts | 872 | 36 |
| Missed reference opportunities | 0/4 | 0/4 |
| Exclusive-direction changes | 44 | 3 |
| Opposite or flat price movement four hours after a change | 13/44 | 3/3 |
| Resolved events in the separate cost-model comparison | 32 | 18 |
| Mean net return on modeled trade notional | -0.3033% | -0.2362% |

The reference set requires V18.3.1 entry status, a limit penetration and a profitable target-first resolution within 12 hours on the archived one-minute path. A timestamp and direction match is required. When both directions are notified together, that timestamp is excluded from the direction-change denominator. The cost comparison uses the same V18.3.1 price templates for emitted matched events, 10 bp combined fees, 5 bp stop slippage and stop priority in a minute. No funding history was applied. This is an exploratory sample used during development and does not prove improved profitability or a lower flip error rate. It is not a portfolio backtest.

Registered positions remain local user records. Existing stop lines are proposals only and are never loosened by the adjustment function. Live quote smoke check found BTC and ETH with daily/four-hour direction and no missing inputs. Windows installation and Telegram delivery require the user's client.
